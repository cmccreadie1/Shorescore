Competition Rules, User Guide & Release Changelog

1. Team Separation & Draw Mechanics: No two anglers from the same 4-person team are assigned to the same zone on the same day. Day 2 zone rotation follows the verified 2-Zone leap strategy (Red $\rightarrow$ Green, Yellow $\rightarrow$ Blue).

2. Mobility Assistance [A] Pegs: Anglers marked as mobility assisted [A] are strictly allocated to pre-designated accessible pegs (e.g., parking adjacent or flat ground) for both competition days.

3. Scoring & Tie-Breakers: Match standings are calculated on Zone Points first (1 point for 1st in zone, 2 for 2nd, etc.). Ties are broken by total length in centimeters (CM), followed by total fish count, and then biggest specimen fish.

4. Blanking & Disqualifications: Anglers recording zero fish in a session receive zone points equal to the total number of pegs in that zone for the session.

1. Enter match title, select 1 or 2 day duration, specify number of teams/solos, and click Apply Structure.

2. Fill in angler names and check mobility [A] boxes if applicable.

3. Click CREATE THE DRAW ⚡ to execute the verified Monte Carlo allocation.

4. Use the Live Workspace tabs to enter scorecards, view standings, or export JSON results.
